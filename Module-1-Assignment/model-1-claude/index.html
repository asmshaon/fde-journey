How an engagement runs

From first call to handover.

A typical engagement takes 8 to 12 weeks. Every phase ends with something you can check, so you always know if it's working.

  1. Week 0

    Scope

    We talk with the people who own the problem and the people who live with it. Then we pick one workflow where shipping would make a measurable difference.

    You get: a one-page plan with a success metric
  2. Weeks 1–2

    Embed

    Engineers join your standups, your Slack and your repos. We shadow the work, get access to real data and build an eval set with your experts.

    You get: a baseline and a first prototype on real data
  3. Weeks 3–8

    Build

    We ship to production behind a shadow mode first, then expand as the numbers hold. Weekly demos to whoever wants to see them.

    You get: a live system, monitored against the metric
  4. Weeks 9+

    Hand over

    Your engineers take the pager. We pair on fixes, write the runbook and stay on call for 30 days after we leave.

    You get: a system your team owns outright

Questions

Before you book.

What is a forward deployed engineer?

A senior software engineer who works inside a customer's organisation instead of from a vendor's office. They write production code, but they also sit with operators, untangle access and data problems, and decide what to build based on what they see. It's part engineer, part consultant, and accountable for the result.

Do your engineers need to be on site?

For the first two weeks, we strongly prefer it — that's when we learn how the work really happens. After that, most teams settle on one or two days on site and the rest remote. Fully remote engagements are possible if your team is remote too.

Who owns the code?

You do, from the first commit. Everything lives in your repositories and runs in your cloud accounts. We don't license a platform back to you.

How do you handle security and data access?

We work inside your security boundary under your policies, with named engineers, least-privilege access and your audit logging. We sign your NDA and DPA, and can go through your vendor security review before day one.

Which models and tools do you use?

Whatever fits your constraints. We regularly ship with the major hosted model providers and open-weight models running in your own cloud, and we choose based on your evals, latency and cost — not a partnership.

What happens if the project isn't working?

Every phase has a metric agreed up front. If we miss it, we'll tell you in the weekly demo, explain why, and recommend whether to change course or stop. Monthly engagements can be ended with 30 days' notice.
